A fresh start with financial independence is possible

When personal and family situations change, financial security can end up taking a back seat to safety, housing and work. Melissa helps people rebuild financial confidence after big life changes, such as divorce or leaving domestic violence.

Combining her own life experience with her professional skills, Melissa is passionate about helping people regain control of their finances, one step at a time. She works alongside family lawyers, community organisations, counsellors, mediators and conveyancers to provide holistic support.

Remember, you're healing, you're becoming stronger, you're not alone.
